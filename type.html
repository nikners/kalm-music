Going grotesque — Fraunces vs Figtree vs Inter

The whole display role. Kalm currently sets titles in Fraunces and body in Spectral; both references (TASTE and Melius) use a grotesque throughout, so this is the decision that decides whether Kalm reads as an archival record sleeve or a tool. Weights are measured, not estimated.
